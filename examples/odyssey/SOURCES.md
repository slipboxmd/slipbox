# Sources — The Odyssey

A corpus about Homer's *Odyssey*: several translations, plus **public-domain
scholarship, commentary, essays, and retellings across the history of Homer
criticism** (~1858–1930). All in the public domain and downloadable as text or
PDF — mostly from [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org) and the
[Internet Archive](https://archive.org).

> **Downloading:** Gutenberg → grab the `.txt`. Internet Archive → the full text
> is at `archive.org/stream/<id>/<id>_djvu.txt`, or download the PDF from the
> item page. Drop the file into `sources/`. (A few Archive items are "borrow
> only" scans — check the item has a Full-Text/PDF download before relying on it.)
